Welcome to the Students Study Planner Web App! This application is designed to help students organize their study schedules and manage their time effectively by providing a simple and intuitive interface. With this app, you can create personalized study plans, set goals, and track your progress.

Instructions on how to use the app:

  • Navigate to the "Tasks and deadlines" section to manage your study schedule.
  • Click on "Review" to share your feedback about the app.

How to Use The Study Planner

  • Add a Task
  • Type your task or assignment in the input bar
  • Click Save task
  • Track Your Progress
  • Check your progress bar to see how much you’ve completed
  • Switch to Dark Mode
  • Click the Dark Mode icon in the top-left corner
  • Click again to switch back to Light Mode
  • Tips to study easy
  • Add tasks daily to stay organized
  • Break large assignments into smaller tasks
  • Review your planner before studying each day
  • Click on "Review" to share your feedback about the app.
